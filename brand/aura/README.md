# AURA | أورا — Brand Identity

Brand book and logo assets for AURA, a Saudi ride-hailing app.

- `brand-book.html`: the full identity presentation (Arabic RTL, with an EN toggle): essence, logo and rules, app icon, color, type, patterns, 12 app screens, fleet, captain uniform and ID, marketing.
- `aura-logo-*.svg`, `aura-symbol-*.svg`, `aura-app-icon*.svg`: vector assets. The wordmark is a vector trace of the approved logo artwork, used unchanged.
- `src/build.mjs`: generates all of the above. Run `node brand/aura/src/build.mjs`.

Palette: #101112 black · #FFFFFF white · #78B83F motion green · #F3F4F2 road gray.
Fonts: Readex Pro (display), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (text), from Google Fonts.
